About Us

Empowering Expectant Mothers Across Every Stage

At Jannani, we believe every expectant mother deserves to feel supported, informed, and safe — every step of the way.

Born from a simple but powerful idea, "Jannani" (meaning mother) was created to be more than just an app; it's a dedicated companion for the pregnancy journey.

We understand that pregnancy brings both joy and uncertainty. That's why we built a platform that brings together everything an expectant mother needs: instant access to nearby emergency hospitals, a trusted network of gynecologists, yoga instructors, nutritionists, and a caring community of women walking the same path.

Our mission is to make quality maternal care accessible, personal, and reassuring — putting critical information and emergency support right at your fingertips, whenever you need it.
